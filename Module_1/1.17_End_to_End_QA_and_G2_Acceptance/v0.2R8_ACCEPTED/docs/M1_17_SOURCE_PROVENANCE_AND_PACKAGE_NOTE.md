# M1.17 Source Provenance and Packaging Note

The accepted database result is supported by the complete live execution evidence and the exact source artifacts available in the active runtime.

The final package preserves:

- the original uploaded M1.17 v0.2 source package;
- all locally available hotfix source packages from v0.2R1 and v0.2R3 through v0.2R8;
- the exact final v0.2R6 acceptance source and v0.2R8 detailed-report source;
- every supplied execution CSV and final report export;
- the original failure and recovery history.

The exact standalone v0.2R2 source files for Programs 124C through 128 were not present in the active filesystem at final packaging. They are represented by their live execution evidence, revision history, and the original-plus-hotfix source chain rather than being silently regenerated or falsely labeled as byte-identical executed source. This packaging limitation does not affect the database acceptance determination, but it is recorded for complete source provenance.
