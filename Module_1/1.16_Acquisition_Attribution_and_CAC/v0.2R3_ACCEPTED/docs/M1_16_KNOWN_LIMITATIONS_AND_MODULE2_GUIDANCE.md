# M1.16 Known Limitations and Module 2 Guidance

M1.16 uses synthetic demonstration assumptions. It does not represent actual vendor invoices, accounting expense, production transfer pricing, causal attribution, funding conversion, funded CAC, payback, or lifetime value.

Module 2 may later combine funding probability, accepted conditional contribution, incremental conditional acquisition cost, and already incurred acquisition cost. It must keep economic segmentation separate from credit eligibility and conduct explicit fairness/steering governance before any channel attribute affects decision strategy.
