# M1.16 Cost Basis and Timing Methodology

## Timing
- `INCURRED_PRE_APPLICATION`
- `INCURRED_AT_APPLICATION`
- `CONDITIONAL_ON_APPROVAL`
- `CONDITIONAL_ON_ACCEPTANCE`
- `CONDITIONAL_ON_FUNDING`

Already incurred/allocated costs remain separate from contingent booking costs.

## Basis
The governed dictionary supports fixed campaign, delivery/impression, click/response, qualified lead, application start, submitted application, future approval/acceptance/funding bases, percent funded amount, internal labor hour, allocated overhead, supported zero, not applicable, and governed parameter.

M1.16 does not fabricate approval, acceptance, or funded counts. Conditional basis definitions are retained only as future-payable evidence.
