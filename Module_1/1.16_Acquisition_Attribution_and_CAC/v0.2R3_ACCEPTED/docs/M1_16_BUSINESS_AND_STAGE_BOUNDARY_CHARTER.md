# M1.16 Business and Stage-Boundary Charter

## Authorized question
Through what governed source, campaign, partner, and interaction path did each accepted application arrive; what acquisition costs were incurred or would become payable; how reliable is that evidence; and how does detailed cost overlap with accepted M1.14?

## Authorized outputs
Source profiles, campaigns, aggregate funnel evidence, touchpoints, attribution, acquisition-cost ledger and components, application-level cost snapshots, companion latest/archive contracts, read-only integrated views, and governed evidence.

## Prohibited outputs
Pricing, factor/rate, approved amount, approval/decline/counteroffer/manual-review decisions, funded outcomes or probabilities, realized CAC/payback/LTV, marketing optimization, causal attribution, accounting conclusions, legal/fair-lending conclusions, PII, device/cookie/ad IDs, or new credit-risk attributes.

## Credit-policy boundary
Acquisition source and cost are economic and operational evidence. They must not be used here as a proxy for creditworthiness or adverse action.
