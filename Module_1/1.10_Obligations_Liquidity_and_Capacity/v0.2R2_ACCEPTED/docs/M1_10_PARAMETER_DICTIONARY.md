# M1.10 Parameter Dictionary

The governed policy profile is `M1_10_OBLIGATIONS_LIQUIDITY_CAPACITY` version 1.

Key parameter groups:

- obligation-count distribution and prior-advance minimum;
- requested-burden basis and monthly/liquidity horizons;
- coverage, burden-rate, and liquidity-buffer tier thresholds;
- stacking and concentration review thresholds;
- industry operating-cash-margin proxies;
- obligation-type balance, finance-factor, and term assumptions;
- stress capacity-tier floor.

The complete machine-readable dictionary and values are in `catalogs/M1_10_PARAMETER_DICTIONARY.csv` and `catalogs/M1_10_PARAMETER_VALUES.csv`.
