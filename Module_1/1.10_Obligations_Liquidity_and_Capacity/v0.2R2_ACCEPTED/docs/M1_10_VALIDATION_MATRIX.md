# M1.10 Validation Matrix

M1.10 contains 70 blocking positive controls and 6 fail-closed negative controls.

Positive controls cover:

- accepted predecessor gates and hashes;
- exact application/scenario cardinality and unique grains;
- obligation row domains, amounts, source lineage, and deterministic hashes;
- requested burden identity;
- existing/total burden identities;
- operating cash, coverage, residual cash, and liquidity identities;
- capacity tier, stress floor, affordability, fallback, and manual-review logic;
- matched baseline/stress coverage;
- canonical set hashes;
- stage boundaries and blocking errors.

Negative controls prove rejection of disabled policy, invalid methodology, invalid burden basis, unapproved stress scenario, prerequisite drift, and post-generation rerun.
