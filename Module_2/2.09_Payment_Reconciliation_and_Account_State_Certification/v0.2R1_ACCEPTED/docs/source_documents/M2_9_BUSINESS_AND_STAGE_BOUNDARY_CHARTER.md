# M2.9 Business and Stage-Boundary Charter

## Authorized

- consume accepted M2.8 account, event, and transition evidence;
- reconcile scheduled, processed, returned, and retry amounts;
- independently reconstruct ending exposure;
- identify and resolve the synthetic return exception;
- certify closed, reassessment-due, and review-hold account states;
- publish latest/archive, exception-ledger, comparison, lineage, and dashboard evidence.

## Prohibited

- real funds movement;
- bank-account, routing, settlement, or ACH data;
- payment-network transmission or external processor calls;
- accounting or servicing-ledger posting;
- external account-state updates or reporting submissions;
- merchant contact;
- write-off, collection, or legal execution;
- external notice or production adverse action;
- modification of accepted M2.8 history.
