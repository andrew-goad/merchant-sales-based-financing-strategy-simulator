# M2.6 — Early Warning, Intervention & Servicing Strategy

M2.6 consumes the accepted M2.5 daily remittance, exposure and portfolio monitoring contract and produces recommendation-only early-warning intervention and servicing strategy outputs.

It creates source snapshots, strategy snapshots, scenario portfolio summaries, latest/archive contracts, validation evidence, acceptance evidence, reports, lineage and Power BI views.

M2.6 does not execute merchant contact, payment changes, restructures, write-offs, charge-offs, collections, legal action, external notices or production adverse-action notices.

## Boundary

All actions are recommendation-only. Execution remains downstream and out of scope.
