# M2.7 — Workout, Restructure, Controlled Exit & Recovery Authorization

M2.7 is authorized for governed development after formal M2.6 acceptance.

M2.7 should consume the accepted recommendation-only M2.6 contract and govern:

- workout and restructure eligibility;
- temporary-term authorization;
- controlled exit and recovery review;
- cure and reassessment requirements;
- approval authorities and manual governance;
- executed-versus-recommended action separation;
- external communication and legal/compliance boundaries;
- latest/archive contracts, negative controls, and acceptance evidence.

M2.7 must preserve accepted M2.5 monitoring history and accepted M2.6
recommendation history. An M2.6 recommendation must not be represented as an
executed merchant contact, payment change, write-off, collection action, legal
action, or external notice.
