# M2.1 Architecture

M2.1 consumes only the accepted G2 integrated contract. It materializes the 1,500-row source once, evaluates 12 transparent gates, aggregates the independent route, applies a matched adverse-scenario non-improvement floor, and publishes a versioned latest/archive routing contract.

Acquisition source and cost evidence are operational/economic evidence only. They can route a record to manual review when blocked, but cannot produce a policy decline.
