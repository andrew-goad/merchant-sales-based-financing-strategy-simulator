# M2.1 Design and Generation Specification

## Business purpose
Determine whether an accepted application/scenario record is eligible to continue to offer design, requires manual review, lacks sufficient evidence, or fails a governed policy gate.

## Routing priority
`DECLINE_POLICY` > `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` > `MANUAL_REVIEW` > `ELIGIBLE_FOR_OFFER_DESIGN`.

## Scenario treatment
The stress route cannot improve relative to the matched baseline route. Underlying G2 evidence remains unchanged.

## Stage boundary
M2.1 is not a final credit approval and does not create offer terms. M2.2 will require the additional certified requested-structure fields needed for pricing and counteroffer design.
