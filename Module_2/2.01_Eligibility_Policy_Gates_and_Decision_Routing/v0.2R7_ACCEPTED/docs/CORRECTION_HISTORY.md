# Correction and Supersession History — M2.1

## Public disposition

The accepted stage may include bounded corrections, hotfixes, recoveries, or supersession records. These records are retained as provenance; they do not reopen the accepted business design.

- Current executable SQL is published only under `src/current/` and `src/reporting/`.
- Recovery SQL is isolated under `src/recovery/`.
- Superseded SQL is prohibited from the public normal-chain folders.
- Correction records must preserve the accepted stage semantics and identify the superseded authority.
