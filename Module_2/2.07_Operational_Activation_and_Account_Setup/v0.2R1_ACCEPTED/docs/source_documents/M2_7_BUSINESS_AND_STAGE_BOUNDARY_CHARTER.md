# M2.7 Business and Stage-Boundary Charter

## Authorized

- consume accepted M2.6 strategy recommendations;
- classify synthetic operational setup posture;
- create synthetic case, setup, and plan identifiers;
- create synthetic effective and reassessment dates;
- carry temporary review terms into a synthetic blueprint;
- publish latest/archive, lineage, comparison, and reporting evidence.

## Not authorized

- production core-account creation;
- production payment or remittance change;
- bank-account or routing data;
- ACH or payment-network transmission;
- merchant contact;
- write-off or charge-off posting;
- collection placement or legal action;
- external or production adverse-action notices;
- rewriting accepted M2.6 history.
