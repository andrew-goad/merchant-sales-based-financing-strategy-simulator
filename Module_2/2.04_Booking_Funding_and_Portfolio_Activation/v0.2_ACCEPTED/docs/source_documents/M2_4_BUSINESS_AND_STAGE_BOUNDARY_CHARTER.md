# M2.4 Business and Stage-Boundary Charter

## Authorized

- scenario-specific synthetic offer acceptance assumption;
- synthetic booking and funding authorization;
- deterministic synthetic account and advance identifiers;
- synthetic booking, funding, activation, first-remittance and monitoring dates;
- initial portfolio position and balance/exposure proxies;
- internal-only notice controls;
- latest/archive contracts, lineage, evidence and Power BI views.

## Prohibited

- real funds movement;
- ACH trace, bank account, routing or settlement account information;
- core-system booking identifiers or real account numbers;
- external notice transmission;
- production adverse-action notices;
- rewriting accepted M2.3 final decisions;
- realized remittance or performance outcomes.

`BOOKED_FUNDED_PORTFOLIO_ACTIVATED` is a synthetic simulation outcome, not evidence that a real merchant accepted an offer or that real funds moved.
