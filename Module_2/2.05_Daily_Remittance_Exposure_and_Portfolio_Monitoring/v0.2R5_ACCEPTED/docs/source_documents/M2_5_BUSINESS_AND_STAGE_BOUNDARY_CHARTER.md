# M2.5 Business and Stage-Boundary Charter

Authorized: deterministic synthetic remittance calculation, expected-versus-actual pace, receivable exposure, principal and unearned-finance-charge proxies, liquidity context, internal monitoring status, alerts, latest/archive contracts, portfolio summaries, lineage and Power BI consumption views.

Not authorized: real debit/payment-network instructions, merchant contact, collections action, servicing action, write-off, charge-off, restructure, workout offer, external notice, production adverse-action notice, or legal/compliance conclusion.
