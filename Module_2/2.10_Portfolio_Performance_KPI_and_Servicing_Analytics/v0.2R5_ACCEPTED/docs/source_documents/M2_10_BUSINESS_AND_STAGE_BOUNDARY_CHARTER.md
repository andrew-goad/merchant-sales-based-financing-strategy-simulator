# M2.10 Business and Stage-Boundary Charter

## Authorized

- consume accepted M2.9 certified accounts;
- classify deterministic performance tiers and queues;
- calculate portfolio and scenario KPIs;
- quantify synthetic servicing burden;
- publish latest/archive, comparison, lineage, and dashboard evidence.

## Prohibited

- production approval, pricing, servicing, or collection decisions;
- real account updates or funds movement;
- external system changes;
- merchant contact;
- write-off, collection, or legal execution;
- external notice or production adverse action;
- modification of accepted M2.9 history.
