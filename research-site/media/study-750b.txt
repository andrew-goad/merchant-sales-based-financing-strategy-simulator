Merchant Sales-Based Financing | 750 · Local GUI
One merchant, two repayment paths
Film duration 1:35

Narration

Could a share of daily sales repay this equipment repair in 120 days?

I selected Local Windows and generated 750 simulated merchants. I submitted one lending policy. The run evaluated every application under both Baseline and Recession Energy.

About two hours later, the financial checks passed. The console listed this run’s Tableau and Power BI workbooks.

Tableau pairs each merchant’s two decisions. 115 businesses received financing in Baseline. 35 also received financing under stress.

Power BI shows both contracts for merchant 462. In each, the business received $146,600, with the same repayment terms.

Just under ten cents of each eligible sales dollar went toward repayment. On day three, zero eligible sales meant a zero payment.

For 60 days, the balances match. Then the model starts replaying sales dates affected by the energy shock. Baseline pays off on day 111. Under stress, over $3,700 is still due on day 120.

All 35 merchants funded in both scenarios paid off in Baseline. Under stress, 34 did. This equipment repair merchant accounts for the difference.
