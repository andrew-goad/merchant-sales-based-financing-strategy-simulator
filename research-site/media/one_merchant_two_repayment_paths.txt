Who gets financed? When does the advance pay off?
Two-minute film, 750 · Local GUI
Descriptive transcript of the silent edition

00:00–00:05 | The question
A simulated business needs $146,600 for equipment repair. The opening shows its application, the lender’s decision and a chart of the next 120 days.

00:05–00:25 | Set up the experiment
In the campaign console the operator selects Local Windows, generates 750 synthetic merchants and chooses the Baseline experiment role to establish a reference policy. Every application is evaluated under both Baseline and Recession Energy. The operator reviews the policy, merchant count and output location before submission.

00:25–00:30 | Read the return
The console reports that the calculation passed financial acceptance. It lists the report and workbook locations. The subsequent Power BI views come from the amended desktop review of this run.

00:30–00:41 | Who has a path to financing?
The Power BI comparison shows 321 supportable applications in Baseline, including 115 funded and 206 referred for review. Under Recession Energy, 94 were supportable, including 35 funded and 59 referred. The denominator is 750 applications in each scenario.

00:41–01:05 | What changed in the offer?
The Tableau matrix pairs the same applications across scenarios. For 241 of 750, the decision category changed. In the selected cell, merchant 000703 received the requested $11,300 in Baseline and an authorized $11,200 counteroffer under stress. The other 34 merchants funded in both scenarios received the same principal in each.

01:05–01:24 | How is the daily payment calculated?
Power BI opens merchant 000462’s equipment-repair request. Both scenarios fund $146,600 on the same terms. The merchant remits 9.7672% of eligible daily sales until the $184,463.41 total due is repaid. Day one’s eligible sales of $24,302.06 produce a $2,373.63 payment. On day three, eligible sales and payment both equal zero.

01:24–01:42 | Follow the outstanding balance
The balance lines coincide through day 60, then diverge. Baseline reaches zero on day 111. Under Recession Energy, $3,743.44 remains on day 120, the last day observed.

01:42–01:52 | Where does this case fit?
Thirty-five merchants received funding in both scenarios. Of these, 28 repaid on the same day, five later under stress and one earlier under stress. Merchant 000462 was the one still carrying a balance under stress on day 120.

01:52–02:00 | Try another experiment
The console illustrates using the saved population as a Challenger. In the example the user changes one rule, the review cost guardrail from 2% to 0%. The screen presents a proposed experiment. Its results would come from a separate submission.
