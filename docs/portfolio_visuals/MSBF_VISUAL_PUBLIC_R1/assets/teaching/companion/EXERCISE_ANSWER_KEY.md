# Exercise answers — 22-page R8 masterclass

The following is the exact question/answer pair printed on each page. Expanded explanation is in the learning guide.

## 01 · Understanding the Mechanics Behind Merchant POS Lending

**Try it:** Why is a strong sales total not enough to approve an advance?

**Answer:** Sales must support the requested burden after obligations, liquidity, risk, economics and authority tests.

## 02 · Read the merchant—not just the sales total

**Try it:** Similar sales, different supportable amounts: which drivers could explain the difference?

**Answer:** Obligations and modeled margin alter residual cash; liquidity, evidence, risk and request terms also matter.

## 03 · Sales tell a pattern—not only a total

**Try it:** Can four raw percentages be added to reproduce the revenue score?

**Answer:** No. Scale and clip each feature first. Then apply 40% / 25% / 20% / 15%; missing inputs can block it.

## 04 · Build capacity from cash and commitments

**Try it:** Why is $1,717.45 observed balance different from the $3,218.65 projected buffer?

**Answer:** The model adds 30 days of residual cash. The projection is not an observed balance or an advance deposit.

## 05 · Before judging performance, establish evidence

**Try it:** Can a smaller request repair 738’s missing eligible-sales feature?

**Answer:** No. Revised terms cannot supply missing observations. Required evidence must exist before dependent tests.

## 06 · Risk and economics answer different questions

**Try it:** Does clearing the economic hurdle compensate for a capacity failure?

**Answer:** No. Capacity and economics are separate required tests. Strong contribution cannot waive another gate.

## 07 · Read the request as four linked parameters

**Try it:** At $1,500 eligible sales and a $100 balance, what is a 10% remittance?

**Answer:** $100. The share is $150, but min($150, $100) = $100. All assumptions belong to this illustration.

## 08 · Change one term. Find the binding burden.

**Try it:** Why does reducing the remittance rate to 8% leave N unchanged?

**Answer:** The $72.75 horizon leg still exceeds the $33.86 sales-share leg. The larger amount governs.

## 09 · Fit the structure inside the capacity boundary

**Try it:** Does a large bank balance prove merchant 082’s request is affordable?

**Answer:** No. Requested residual cash is −$471.87/day; coverage and burden fail despite a large buffer.

## 10 · What can revised terms fix—and what can’t they?

**Try it:** Why does 738’s broad verification REVIEW label not explain its stress evidence decline?

**Answer:** Its refined human gate is NONE. Stress separately lacks 30 usable POS days; that specific evidence failure binds.

## 11 · Search a governed set—not an unlimited menu

**Try it:** Can higher modeled return admit a candidate below the 40% amount floor?

**Answer:** No. Policy admissibility and feasibility come before preference. The objective cannot waive a boundary.

## 12 · Candidate lab: where the boundary moves

**Try it:** Why does the $2,500 candidate fail despite 1.00× coverage?

**Answer:** It is 4.65% of the $53,800 request—below the 40% floor ($21,520). Passing capacity cannot override it.

## 13 · A supportable structure still needs authority

**Try it:** Would raising coverage to 1.25× remove every reason for review?

**Answer:** Not necessarily. MEDIUM_DATA_CONFIDENCE remains, and every other strict condition must still pass.

## 14 · Seven outcomes—and the reason behind each

**Try it:** Can “no feasible structure” follow an independent review candidate?

**Answer:** Yes. For 082 stress, the matched-stress floor leaves no eligible final candidate. Independent support is not final authority.

## 15 · The preferred candidate is not always the final one

**Try it:** Why can’t 082 stress or 738 stress simply copy 098’s lower amount?

**Answer:** 082 loses eligible candidates under the stress floor. 738 lacks required evidence. Neither is repaired by copying a term.

## 16 · Follow the exact path into the account

**Try it:** What happens when the stress-declined 738 path is joined only on merchant ID?

**Answer:** The Baseline account could leak into the stress path. Use the complete run / scenario / application key.

## 17 · What does active monitoring actually watch?

**Try it:** Can day-8 liquidity create a watch signal before pace becomes eligible?

**Answer:** Yes. Liquidity starts at day 7 under its own conditions. The 21/30/3 rule applies to pace—not all signals.

## 18 · Pace is a benchmark plus persistence

**Try it:** Why can 66.99% qualify on day 32 while a poor earlier ratio did not?

**Answer:** Adverse pace needs three eligible breaches from day 30. Earlier ratios earn no persistence credit; other rules still apply.

## 19 · One account. Several signals. One governed status.

**Try it:** At day 120, why does 78.09% pace not make the account merely Watch?

**Answer:** The unpaid 90-day horizon has been exceeded. Horizon severity governs; pace and liquidity signals still remain.

## 20 · A recommendation is not permission to execute

**Try it:** Does a targeted-outreach recommendation prove that an action happened?

**Answer:** No. The three outreach paths remain held. Read setup permission, processing permission and the execution result separately.

## 21 · Reconcile the event—and the receivable

**Try it:** Can zero unresolved exceptions coexist with a $6,061.03 receivable?

**Answer:** Yes. Reconciliation establishes agreement of events and balances. Payoff is a different question: is the receivable zero?

## 22 · Turn a portfolio number back into an explanation

**Try it:** Which denominator answers authorization—and which answers payoff?

**Answer:** Authorization uses all 750 paths in the named scenario. Payoff uses its funded subset: 103 or 27.


## 23. Closing synthesis — teach it forward

**Final Try It:** Choose one merchant case. Explain its outcome without saying “because the model said so.”

**Answer / teach-back check:** Name the inputs, the binding rule, the permitted action and the account evidence. An absence of funded membership is itself an important result when explaining a review or decline. This is a recap, not a new source calculation.
