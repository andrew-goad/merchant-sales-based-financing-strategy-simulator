WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE DECISION
From synthetic funding and monitoring to permitted action, portfolio insight and certification.

1 — BOOKING & ACTIVATION
Only an authorized, physically funded scenario path enters the account lifecycle.

Authorized terms
Selected amount and governed financing terms.

Path identity
Run, scenario, application, account and advance.

Synthetic funding
Recorded funding—not a real transfer.

Source handoff
Funding dates, keys and retained decision evidence.

Account activation
The exact funded path enters monitoring.

2 — DAILY MONITORING LOGIC
On unpaid paths, overlapping signals feed source-defined monitoring status.

OVERLAPPING MONITORING FAMILIES
No remittance
Elapsed days without positive remittance.

Sales dormancy
Consecutive zero-sales days.

Liquidity
Balance, NSF events and negative-balance evidence.

Pace
Cumulative remittance versus capped expectation.

Horizon
Unpaid balance beyond the selected horizon.

NON-CURRENT MONITORING STATUSES
WATCH
Watch-level condition; no higher-priority status.

UNDERPERFORMING
Adverse pace; no overriding status.

SEVERE SHORTFALL
Severe pace or unpaid horizon overrun.

DORMANT / NO REMITTANCE
Source no-remittance threshold reached.
