How the Governed
Decision Engine Works
A proof-style walkthrough of the merchant financing engine

1 — INPUTS
Merchant evidence and requested terms feed derived assessments.

Merchant context
Industry and relationship; policy context.

Requested structure
Amount, remittance, payback multiple and horizon.

Sales & cash flow
Sales, settlement, deposits and usable history.

Obligations & liquidity
Cash balances and existing payment commitments.

Verification & continuity
Source confidence, verification and processor continuity.

Derived risk & economics
Synthetic comparative risk, loss, costs and contribution.

Source inputs and derived assessments stay distinct; retain run and source identity.

2 — ELIGIBILITY GATES
Different conditions require different routes—not one generic failure.

Evidence sufficiency
Required inputs must be usable. Missing is not zero.

Policy hard stops
Non-remediable conditions remain declines.

Structure conditions
Capacity and economics can require a search.

Judgment requirements
Defined review conditions can remain after search.

Advisories
Retain warnings; apply the specific source rule.

Terms cannot repair missing evidence or waive hard stops.

3 — STRUCTURE SEARCH
M2.2 constructs and tests. M2.3 resolves final authority.

Horizon
30 / 60 / 90 days

Remittance
Sales-linked share

Amount
Advance dollars

Payback multiple
Repayment target factor

Economic hurdle
Required modeled contribution test

POLICY GUARDRAILS
Minimum counteroffer = 40% of requested amount
Maximum amount reduction = 60%

Test admissible terms, credit and economics together; final authority remains separate.

4 — DECISION CIRCUIT
Structure support and final authority are separate tests.

Structure support
Requested or alternative terms satisfy the applicable tests.
↓
Final authority / M2.3
Strict or review conditions + matched-scenario floor.
→ ROUTES TO OUTCOMES

Support ≠ authorization.

1. Approve as requested
Requested terms satisfy governing tests and authority.

2. Counteroffer authorized
Alternative terms satisfy governing tests and authority.

3. Counteroffer review required
Selected alternative; binding review requirements remain.

4. Manual underwriting review
Source-defined underwriting judgment remains required.

5. Evidence decline
Required inputs cannot support the dependent assessment.

6. No feasible structure
No eligible final structure after search and governance.

7. Policy hard stop
A non-remediable policy condition controls.

Retain the final outcome, applicable terms and binding reasons.
