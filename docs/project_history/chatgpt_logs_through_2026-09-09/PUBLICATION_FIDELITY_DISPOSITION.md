# Public-history fidelity disposition

**Source-owner disposition: FROZEN_VISIBLE_CORPUS_WITH_DISCLOSED_FIDELITY_LIMITATIONS.**

The archive preserves the substantive available dialogue rather than replacing it with a short chronology. Exact structured records, normalized visible text, page-image/OCR transcription, source-labeled summaries, attachment/image events and incomplete source regions remain distinguishable. Markdown/JSONL parity concerns this canonical population; it is not proof that an export captured every platform message or that every OCR glyph is exact.

Text hidden outside horizontal scroll areas and collapsed attachment tables is not reconstructed. Source filenames/hashes and available source times remain traceable; copied decision bodies are references to their canonical event, not additional Independent Audit decisions.

The Part3 continuation retains its declared 24 visible-context, 12 detailed nonverbatim, one source-referenced audit-paste, four normalized and four image-event records. Later PDF wording is linked where supplied; no original record is silently overwritten.
