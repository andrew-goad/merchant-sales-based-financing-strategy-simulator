### I would preserve the ten-page structure, but sharpen its story

The pitch should feel like someone asked:

**“We need a better merchant financing decision and portfolio capability. Why should we trust you to design it?”**

Then ten pages answer that question:

1. **The opportunity** — *From First Advance to Intelligent Portfolio.* A visually arresting opening with the merchant-finance mission and perhaps three carefully selected proof points—not ten metrics.

2. **The problem worth solving** — Merchant financing is not simply “approve or decline.” Sales volatility, liquidity, obligations, evidence quality, economics and repayment structure interact. This establishes why simplistic decisioning leaves value on the table.

3. **One connected platform** — A simplified executive interpretation of our newly accepted architecture. Not the architecture poster shrunk onto a slide; a buyer-level journey from merchant evidence → decision → lifecycle → intelligence.

4. **Find the structure, not merely the answer** — This should be one of the signature pages. Show how governed structure search creates a third possibility between “approve exactly what was requested” and “decline.” The 6,519 structures and counter-offer mechanics become commercially intelligible here.

5. **Judgment and authority remain separate** — Explain the difference between finding a supportable alternative and actually authorizing it. This is where the portfolio quietly demonstrates mature credit-risk thinking.

6. **The decision is only the beginning** — Funding, monitoring, intervention, servicing, reconciliation. The **15,600 account-days** become evidence that this is a lifecycle design, not a one-time underwriting exercise.

7. **Turn operations into management intelligence** — Introduce the accepted Power BI experience: portfolio command center, cohort lifecycle, merchant decision intelligence and servicing/performance. Power BI consumes certified facts rather than becoming the decision engine. That distinction is part of the accepted architecture. [export reference: filecite | turn6file12 | L19-L23]
