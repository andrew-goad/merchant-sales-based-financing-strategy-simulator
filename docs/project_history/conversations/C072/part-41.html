Interpretation:

Disposition Meaning
PASS All blocking controls pass; no unresolved material finding
PASS_WITH_OBSERVATIONS Blocking controls pass; only non-blocking improvements remain
HOLD Evidence is incomplete, missing, contradictory, or insufficient
FAIL A material physical contradiction, contract violation, or unauthorized action is proven
NOT_AUDITED Required submission or evidence has not been received
SUPERSEDED An explicitly governed later submission replaced the artifact

A HOLD is not automatically a design failure. It is the correct result when the work may be sound but the evidence does not yet support approval.
