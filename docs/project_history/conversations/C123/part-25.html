1. Storyboard standard

This storyboard treats the report as a portfolio centerpiece, not a technical appendix.

Every page must earn its place by doing at least one of five things:

  1. Show what problem was observed.
  2. Show how human judgment redirected the investigation.
  3. Show how AI accelerated analysis, implementation, or documentation.
  4. Show how independent audit challenged or bounded the result.
  5. Show how the analytical product became stronger, clearer, or more governable.

Screenshots are treated as evidence, not decoration. Editorial design will add order, context, captions, and pacing, but it will not alter screenshot content.
