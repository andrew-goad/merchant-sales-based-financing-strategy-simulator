**We have merchant-application-level strategy simulation, but not profile-driven re-underwriting.**

## Where profiles fit in the logic

The core decision process remains:

```text
Merchant evidence
→ feature availability
→ risk, capacity and request
→ policy gates
→ candidate search
→ selected structure
→ final decision authority
→ economics
→ funding and activation
→ monitoring and intervention
→ account certification
→ portfolio outcome
```

The strategy-profile simulation sits **after the governed source process**. It replays or re-ranks existing candidate and account facts under alternative management objectives; it does not permit Power BI to recreate eligibility, feasibility, authority, funding, or servicing logic. [export reference: filecite | turn2file6 | L279-L299] The final Power BI instructions expressly prohibit DAX or Power Query from reconstructing strategy or lifecycle authority. [export reference: filecite | turn1file4 | L224-L232]

A useful way to think about the distinction is:

```text
Scenario = What operating environment are we in?

Decision engine = What is permitted, declined, or requires review?

Strategy profile = Given the governed possibilities, what objective
                   should drive candidate or account-treatment selection?
```

The profiles are therefore **counterfactual management lenses**, not eight separate underwriting policies.

## The eight profiles are actually three different types

### 1. Control profile

`BASELINE_REPLAY` reproduces the source-selected application and account posture. It is the no-change comparator.

### 2. Application-candidate selection profiles

These six profiles apply different weights to the already-governed candidate population:

- `ACCESS_EXPANSION`
- `PRICE_FOR_RISK`
- `PAYMENT_BURDEN_RELIEF`
- `LOSS_CONTAINMENT`
- `PROFITABILITY_DISCIPLINE`
- `BALANCED_FRONTIER`

They emphasize different combinations of access, selected exposure, finance charge, expected-loss density, contribution, return, and payment burden. The physical profile definitions classify these six as `WEIGHTED_CANDIDATE`; Baseline is `SOURCE_REPLAY`. [export reference: filecite | turn5file0 | L22-L38]

Importantly, the source definition describes **Access Expansion** as testing additional synthetic access only within frozen counteroffer-review and hard-constraint boundaries. Balanced Frontier is explicitly a constraints-first analytical profile and “not automatic approval.” [export reference: filecite | turn5file8 | L347-L356]

### 3. Post-funding account profile

`EARLY_INTERVENTION` is different from the other profiles. It is classified as `RULE_BASED_ACCOUNT`, not candidate scoring. It is a timing-only reassessment or governance-review simulation with unchanged payment factor and exposure. [export reference: filecite | turn5file4 | L164-L167]
