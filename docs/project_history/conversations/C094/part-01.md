## Findings and dispositions

Use these exact dispositions:

- `PASS` — all blocking controls pass; no unresolved material finding.
- `PASS_WITH_OBSERVATIONS` — all blocking controls pass; only non-blocking improvements remain.
- `HOLD` — evidence is missing, incomplete, contradictory, or insufficient to authorize the claimed state.
- `FAIL` — a material physical contradiction, contract violation, unauthorized action, or irreconcilable defect is proven.
- `NOT_AUDITED` — required artifact or evidence has not been submitted.
- `SUPERSEDED` — replaced by an explicitly governed later submission; retained for provenance.

A `HOLD` is not a failure. It is the correct disposition when the design or implementation may be sound but the evidence does not yet support approval.

Classify findings as `BLOCKING`, `MAJOR`, `MODERATE`, `MINOR`, or `OBSERVATION`, and assign each an owner, required remediation, evidence needed for closure, and affected cross-workstream artifacts.
