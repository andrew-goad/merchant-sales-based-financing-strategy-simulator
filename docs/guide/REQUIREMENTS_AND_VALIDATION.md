## Three kinds of evidence—not interchangeable

**Requirements and design** state what a stage owns, what it consumes and what it must preserve. **Validation and negative controls** test those obligations at the relevant grain and failure boundary. **Acceptance/signoff** identifies the actual result/version and its qualifications. A source catalog, screenshot or count of files is not a substitute for any of those.
