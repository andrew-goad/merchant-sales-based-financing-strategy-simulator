# M1.6 · Matched POS and deposit scenarios

**How can stress be compared without changing the underlying merchants?**

[Stage map](../../README.md#stage-map) · [Which source version?](../SOURCE_VERSIONS.md) · [Current use](../CURRENT_USE.md)

> **Source basis:** original `v0.2R3_ACCEPTED` in `Module_1/1.06_Matched_POS_and_Deposit_Scenarios`. This is the retained foundation/Module 1 basis; no Module 1 technical payload is changed by the R26 successor. This is a reading companion, not a new technical acceptance or execution guide.

M1.6 applies two governed alternatives to the same accepted merchant/date panel. It isolates scenario differences from population differences and keeps original baseline evidence intact. **Baseline and Recession Energy are alternatives, not a chronology or a forecast.**

## Same keys, separate scenario histories

The comparison key includes scenario, population, merchant and observation date; POS also retains the processor account. Each scenario merchant-day must have both POS and deposit evidence. The two 270,000-row scenario tables contain **540,000 canonical entities** together, not 540,000 merchants.

`BASELINE` copies every accepted M1.4/M1.5 value while adding scenario identity, base-row lineage, overlay payload and hashes. `RECESSION_ENERGY` preserves all pre-shock values and activates its direct overlay in the final 60 days. Dependent-industry propagation begins seven days later, leaving 53 propagated days.

## Direct and propagated effects are explicit

```text
Direct factor = max(1 − direct cap,
                    1 − (1 − scenario sales multiplier) × sensitivity)
Propagated factor = max(1 − propagated cap,
                        1 − (1 − scenario sales multiplier)
                            × energy dependency × damping)
```

An eight-industry matrix records synthetic sensitivities, dependency weights and transmission channels. These are evidence-frozen demonstration assumptions—not estimated industry elasticities.

The POS overlay applies window eligibility, factors, bounded volatility, incremental outage/zero-sales conditions, transaction-quality calculations, eligible sales and lagged settlement. Volatility can vary a particular day up or down; the design does not claim that every individual metric mechanically worsens on every day.

## Carry the balance consequences through

Deposit overlays consume matched scenario POS and the accepted liquidity profile. They recalculate captured deposit, obligations-scaled withdrawal, temporary hold, support logic and balance roll-forward. A matched stress path cannot remove an NSF or negative-balance event already present in Baseline.

Pre-shock deposit and POS values remain exactly equal to the original baseline. The distinction between **scenario selected** and **shock active** is recorded in the overlay, avoiding a false assumption that selecting stress changes every date.

## Handoff to quality and features

Each scenario row retains `base_row_hash`, contract identity, run identity and its own deterministic hash. Full POS, deposit and combined roots bind the accepted result. M1.7 and subsequent features can trace an observation back to its source rather than treating stress as an unrelated new population.

This stage does not calculate final capacity, risk, loss, pricing or decisions. Nor does its matched design demonstrate a causal effect in real merchants. It establishes a controlled comparison surface for those later calculations.

## Requirements interpretation

The following are editorial reading locators into the retained requirements/design/authority documents—not invented native requirement IDs. Native IDs and control lists remain in the linked originals.

**Design §§2–3 · Scenario architecture and transmission.** Keep the fixed panel, approved scenario identities and direct/lagged factors distinct.

**Design §§4–6 · Preservation and matched comparison.** Require exact baseline/pre-shock equality and complete POS/deposit matching.

**Design §7 · Lineage.** Bind each scenario observation to its accepted baseline row and scenario overlay.

## What the retained validation establishes

Accepted v0.2R3 records 62/62 positive controls, 5/5 negatives, 270,000 POS scenario rows, 270,000 deposit scenario rows and zero deterministic mismatches. The source verifies 45,000 direct-window rows and 39,750 propagated-window rows for the stress panel, plus exact pre-shock preservation. Those window populations overlap; they are not additive unique merchants.
