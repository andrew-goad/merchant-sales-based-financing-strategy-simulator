# M1.5 · Daily deposit and liquidity history

**What does the operating account show after settlement, withdrawals and holds?**

[Stage map](../../README.md#stage-map) · [Which source version?](../SOURCE_VERSIONS.md) · [Current use](../CURRENT_USE.md)

> **Source basis:** original `v0.2R2_ACCEPTED` in `Module_1/1.05_Daily_Deposit_and_Liquidity_History`. This is the retained foundation/Module 1 basis; no Module 1 technical payload is changed by the R26 successor. This is a reading companion, not a new technical acceptance or execution guide.

M1.5 translates accepted POS proceeds into a synthetic operating-account path. It preserves deposits, withdrawals, closing balance, available balance, holds and NSF events as different facts. It does not classify a merchant's final capacity or verify a real bank statement.

## A daily balance path, not a sales copy

The grain is **population × merchant × observation date**, producing **135,000 rows** for the same 750 merchants and 180 days. Accepted G1–M1.4 inputs and the `DEPOSIT_DAILY` contract must reconcile before generation.

The merchant liquidity profile resolves capture/withdrawal behavior, initial balance, target buffer, source observability, bounded negative-balance support and optional prior-financing pressure. Its provisional liquidity tier is a synthetic segmentation aid, not a default probability.

## The identities a reader should test

```text
Base POS deposit = accepted net merchant proceeds × daily capture rate
Total deposit = base POS deposit + separately identified support deposit
Closing balance = opening balance + deposits − withdrawals
Available balance = closing balance − temporary hold
Next opening balance = preceding closing balance
```

Capture is bounded between 25% and 100%. Operating withdrawals include ordinary operating demand, bounded stress withdrawals and an assigned existing-financing remittance window. Temporary holds derive from refunds, chargebacks and reversals. These mechanisms explain why sales volume and available liquidity can diverge.

A support deposit appears only when the raw closing path would cross the governed lower balance floor. It is separately visible as synthetic owner/non-POS support; it should not be mistaken for sales revenue or a newly funded MSBF advance.

## Latent truth is not source availability

The full latent synthetic panel is stored even when `deposit_source_available_flag` says the simulated source would not be observable. M1.7 later assesses availability, freshness, completeness and conflict. Merely finding rows in the latent table does not authorize a downstream feature to treat them as usable evidence.

Likewise, the bounded prior-financing remittance pressure here is not the final detailed obligation snapshot, and the NSF logic is not a credit decision. Those responsibilities belong to later stages.

## Preserve the correction and the evidence limitation

The accepted generation correction removed 16 pre-open NSF events while retaining the governed balance history and rebuilding the affected hashes. A later fix qualified an ambiguous read-only detail-report reference without changing accepted business data.

The initial preflight result tab was lost during a DBeaver interruption. The retained signoff explains why acceptance still had sufficient evidence: fail-closed generation readiness, persisted generation evidence, upstream identity preservation, complete expected/actual equality and final controls. This guide does not pretend the missing result tab was recovered.

## What the retained validation establishes

Accepted v0.2R2 records 56/56 positive checks, 4/4 negatives, 135,000 expected/actual rows and zero mismatches. Its report includes 7,598 negative-balance rows and 527 NSF events. These are synthetic evidence observations—not production bank-statement validation or calibrated liquidity behavior.
