# M1.13 · Exposure, recovery and comparative expected loss

**What exposure and recovery assumptions sit underneath a loss number?**

[Stage map](../../README.md#stage-map) · [Which source version?](../SOURCE_VERSIONS.md) · [Current use](../CURRENT_USE.md)

> **Source basis:** original `v0.2R1_ACCEPTED` in `Module_1/1.13_Exposure_Recovery_and_Expected_Loss`. This is the retained foundation/Module 1 basis; no Module 1 technical payload is changed by the R26 successor. This is a reading companion, not a new technical acceptance or execution guide.

M1.13 makes three separate assumptions visible: the exposure path, default timing weights and recovery/LGD treatment. It combines them with M1.12's synthetic risk proxy for comparison, not for an accounting reserve or forecast.

## Principal and contractual receivable are not interchangeable

Initial exposure is the **requested total repayment amount**, not only requested funding. Each application/scenario path runs from day zero through its requested 30-, 60- or 90-day horizon. Governed curve-shape parameters transform the remaining-balance path.

The terminal day is explicitly set to zero in this **origination exposure schedule**, preventing a synthetic penny residual caused by two-decimal daily payment rounding. This assumption must not be imported into later servicing: an observed unpaid M2.5/M2.9 account is not extinguished merely because its horizon has elapsed.

## Time-weighted exposure

Each path day is assigned to EARLY, MIDDLE or LATE timing. The frozen bucket weight is divided across the number of days in that bucket. Path-weighted exposure at default is the sum of ending exposure × daily timing weight.

The resulting weighted EAD is a model-defined synthetic measure. It is not a separately observed default balance or an empirical default-timing distribution.

## Supported recovery credits, bounded LGD

```text
Independent LGD foundation = industry LGD baseline + scenario add-on
                           − supported collateral recovery credit
                           − supported guarantee recovery credit
```

The LGD floor/cap and aggregate recovery-credit cap remain binding. Under the adverse scenario, final LGD cannot improve on matched Baseline. Availability alone does not establish an enforceable recovery; the source records evidence quality and supported credits.

```text
Simple comparative loss = risk proxy × LGD × initial contractual exposure
Schedule-adjusted comparative loss = risk proxy × LGD × path-weighted EAD
```

These formulas answer different exposure questions and must retain their denominator/basis. Neither is CECL, a reserve, a production estimate or realized loss.

## Evidence and handoff

Complete evidence requires usable risk and supported recovery evidence. Partial treatment retains parameter-only or warning-quality recovery assumptions. Blocked integrated risk or a null proxy leaves comparative-loss values null with explicit review routing.

The accepted physical population combines **93,720 daily exposure-path rows and 1,500 snapshots**. M1.14 consumes schedule-adjusted loss in its economics identity. It should not replace the missing value with zero simply to make a contribution number computable.

## Requirements interpretation

The following are editorial reading locators into the retained requirements/design/authority documents—not invented native requirement IDs. Native IDs and control lists remain in the linked originals.

**Design §§3–4 · Exposure and default timing.** Separate contractual starting exposure, path shape and day/bucket weighting.

**Design §§5–7 · Recovery, loss and evidence.** Credit only supported recovery, enforce bounds and retain null losses for blocked evidence.

**Design §8 · Determinism.** Type expected rows to the physical target before comparing persisted hashes.

## What the retained validation establishes

Accepted v0.2R1 records 82/82 positives, 7/7 negatives and zero mismatches across 95,220 canonical entities. EAD, recovery and both loss identities reconcile; matched stress EAD/LGD/loss improvements are zero. The disclosed pre-commit boolean-aggregation defect and governed recovery remain historical provenance.
