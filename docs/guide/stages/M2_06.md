## Recommendations are an explicit product

M2.6 consumes the accepted funded-account monitoring state and produces a governed intervention/servicing recommendation. Its decision is about the next **route**, not an instruction that automatically executes a payment, contacts a merchant or restructures an account.

The current population is 130 funded scenario paths. M2.6–M2.10 each retain that account-path population even when no further action is required. Keeping closed and held states makes the operational boundary inspectable rather than dropping inconvenient cases from the result.

## Read the route, the reason and the permission separately

The original specification maps the source account condition into recommendation/status families and retains action/plan parameters, source identities and contract evidence. M2.7 consumes those recommendations and decides whether setup and processing are allowed. M2.8 then records only the permitted synthetic execution.

A practical reading has three steps: identify the source condition, inspect the recommended response, and follow the permission record. Skipping the second or third relationship turns a governed recommendation into an unsupported claim of automatic servicing.

The current teaching exhibit summarizes **126 closed/no-further-action paths, three review holds and one permitted active-servicing path**. Those are the downstream routes through the connected operational system; they are not three successive phases every account must traverse.

## Why a staircase would be misleading

A picture that forces all accounts through outreach → adjustment → servicing → closure would misstate the demonstrated logic. Paid-off paths can require no new servicing event. Review-recommended outreach paths can remain held. An authorized active path can have actual retained event evidence without proving that every other path was contacted.

The accepted poster and classroom lesson deliberately represent alternatives and permission boundaries. The business lesson is accountability: recommendation, authority and execution must each leave their own record.

## Current versus original evidence

The historical v0.2R1 specification describes 59 paths, including 57 closed, one temporary adjustment and one outreach-review path. The strategy successor expands the funded population to 130. Do not reuse the older 57/1/1 distribution as the current operating picture.

R26 changes monitoring calibration upstream, but the retained R17-to-R26 comparison shows M2.6's 130 rows and 13 shared columns with **zero cell differences**. The downstream nonregression means the accepted calibration did not rewrite the documented intervention output; it is not a claim that all hypothetical future monitoring policies produce identical recommendations.

## Evidence and downstream handoff

Follow the accepted source mapping, intervention outcome/plan fields and hash-bound latest/archive/registry surfaces in the physical stage package. Keep application and scenario attached to the synthetic account. The resulting recommendation is passed to M2.7 alongside the source state it explains.

Any real outreach, collection, modification or funds movement is outside this portfolio's synthetic authority. This documentation does not turn the retained rule branches into production procedures.
