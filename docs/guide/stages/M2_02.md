## How the current search preserves discipline

**Enter search for the right reason.** Decisionable, structure-remediable capacity or economics FAIL/REVIEW paths may enter search. Missing decision-critical evidence and hard policy stops are not cured by changing the terms.

**Construct within the guardrails.** Minimum counteroffer amount is **40% of the request**, maximum amount reduction **60%**, and permitted horizons are **30, 60 or 90 days**. Candidate economics must meet the required hurdle. The profile's automatic total-repayment increase limit is **0%**; the review limit is **2%**. These are policy settings in this source, not universal lending rules.

**Reassess the linked consequences.** Candidate records retain amount/rate/payback/horizon together with capacity, residual cash, projected liquidity, resilience, comparative risk/loss and modeled contribution. Favorable terms alone are not sufficient.

**Select through distinct frontiers, then apply authority.** `DUAL_FRONTIER_AUTHORITY_FIRST` keeps strict and review-eligible candidates distinct. Matched-scenario constraints may require reselection from evaluated candidates. The final governed decision retains the selected candidate, frontier, binding gate/reason codes and stress-floor flags; those explain why structure support did—or did not—become authorization.
