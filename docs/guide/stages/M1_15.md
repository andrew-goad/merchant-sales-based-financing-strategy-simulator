## One interface, three views of time and comparison

The accepted contract is `M1_APPLICATION_CONSUMPTION` v1, schema `M1_CONTRACT_SCHEMA_V1`. It consumes accepted physical M1.8–M1.14 rows rather than regenerating analytics.

| Representation | Accepted grain and population |

| Latest | 1,500 application-scenario rows for current consumption. |
| Immutable archive | 1,500 exact retained contract payloads tied to the accepted run. |
| Matched comparison | 750 application pairs across Baseline and Recession Energy. |
| Contract registry | One versioned identity/lifecycle record. |

The total **3,751 canonical entities** is an evidence reconciliation population, not an application or control count.

## Flatten the useful fields; retain the explanation

High-value fields are flattened for ordinary consumers while source hashes and JSON lineage preserve traceability to their accepted physical owners. A Power BI-oriented consumption view is provided, but no downstream reader is authorized to replace evidence status or invent missing values.

Complete, partial and blocked treatment remains intact. In the accepted latest population, Baseline has 590 partial and 160 blocked rows; Recession Energy has 312 partial and 438 blocked. Those are evidence-contract states. They are not final approval/decline categories.

## Lifecycle status must not change the business identity

Latest and comparison hashes use target-typed physical fields, excluding their row hash and creation timestamp. Archive rows retain the latest contract hash and exact payload. Contract identity excludes lifecycle status, combined hash and timestamps so acceptance can advance lifecycle without changing the immutable contract identity.

This separation prevents an acceptance-time update from appearing to change the underlying business record. It does not authorize deleting inconvenient lineage or treating all metadata as irrelevant to every purpose.

## Two finishing boundaries follow

M1.16 adds an **acquisition companion** at application grain. It does not replace this application/scenario contract. M1.17 then certifies both through the G2 bundle. Keeping the boundaries separate lets a reviewer ask whether an economics/source issue originated in the core evidence or the acquisition bridge.

The archived originals and comparison history remain preserved. The new editorial guide is a reading layer; it neither revises this contract nor reopens its accepted evidence.
