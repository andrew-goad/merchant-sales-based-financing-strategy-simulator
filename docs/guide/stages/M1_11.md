# M1.11 · Cash-flow archetypes and operating resilience

M1.11 interprets already accepted cash-flow and capacity evidence. Its assessed archetype is different from the M1.4 archetype used to generate a synthetic history: one is an input assumption; the other is an evidence-based analytical output.

## Classification is ordered, not a collection of independent badges

The accepted precedence is `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `THIN_HISTORY`, `DISRUPTED`, `DECLINING`, `VOLATILE`, `SEASONAL`, `GROWING`, then `STABLE`.

That ordering prevents an otherwise favorable metric from hiding blocked evidence or a material disruption. Calling a merchant stable simply because one growth measure is positive would bypass the earlier questions in the classification.

M1.9 and M1.10 are consumed as accepted physical inputs. Their observed features and capacity facts are not regenerated here. Operating resilience remains separate from the later integrated credit-risk proxy.

## Governed stress interpretation

The adverse alternative may preserve or worsen the final resilience tier and archetype risk rank, but may not improve either relative to its matched Baseline. The control floors interpreted results without overwriting underlying observed metrics.

The distinction is useful when explaining a result: a stable-looking individual metric does not necessarily overcome earlier evidence limitations, the composite assessment or matched-scenario constraints. The guide must retain both the independent assessment and the governing final interpretation where the source records them.

## What downstream receives

M1.12 consumes these interpretable resilience measures with other evidence to build risk components. M1.11 does not issue a price, approve an offer, create an expected-loss measure or certify a production risk model. Its eight assessed archetypes are not eight strategy profiles; the later M2.11 profile family serves a different analytical purpose.

The source also records a controlled physical extension. The extra tables belong to this later stage; they do not retroactively replace the original G0 catalog acceptance.
