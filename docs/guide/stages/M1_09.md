# M1.9 · As-of cash-flow features

**Which facts were actually available when this application was assessed?**

> **Source basis:** original `v0.2R5_ACCEPTED` in `Module_1/1.09_As_Of_Cash_Flow_Features`. This is the retained foundation/Module 1 basis; no Module 1 technical payload is changed by the R26 successor. This is a reading companion, not a new technical acceptance or execution guide.

M1.9 turns accepted daily evidence into decision-time features. Its central promise is temporal: a trailing window ends at the application's as-of date, and no later observation is allowed to make the application look stronger or weaker.

## Wide consumption and long-form explanation

The source creates **1,500 application-scenario snapshots** and **54,000 long-form values** for 36 governed features. The long form carries feature code/version, source-snapshot lineage, observation window, availability, value or explicit absence, calculation hash and run/scenario identity.

A consumer can use the wide snapshot conveniently while a reviewer traces the feature back to a precise source and window. A matching merchant ID alone is insufficient: the accepted scenario and application identity must also match.

## Time, scale and missingness are part of the calculation

Trailing calendar windows end at `as_of_date`. Scenario differences are matched to the same application in Baseline; Baseline deltas are zero. An unavailable or insufficient feature is `NOT_AVAILABLE`, not synthetic zero.

The accepted annualized-sales basis is **`PERSISTED_ROUNDED_90D_AVERAGE`**. Annualization uses that persisted rounded value and the governed 365-day factor rather than a separately calculated higher-precision average. This keeps the displayed input, stored output and deterministic identity consistent.

The design bounds growth to −1 through 5, coefficients of variation to 0 through 10, and buffer days to −365 through 365. Other seasonality/ratio caps come from policy. Those are governed demonstration calculation limits, not statistical estimates of universal merchant behavior.

## Readiness is not approval

`COMPLETE` means required POS/deposit depth and quality are satisfied. `PARTIAL` preserves controlled limitations. `BLOCKED` includes unavailable POS evidence or M1.8 `STOP`/`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` routing. `ready_for_downstream_flag` permits later feature consumption, not financing.

The accepted source allows portfolio-level adverse direction without forcing every individual observed metric to deteriorate identically. Later stage-specific stress floors govern their own interpretations; they do not erase the underlying feature evidence.

## Why the correction history is informative

The retained chain fixes scenario-ID scope, explicit CTAS projection, target numeric scale, aggregate syntax and annualized-sales identity. The final annualization repair changed 1,472 wide values and their 1,472 corresponding long-form values, with aligned availability and rebuilt hashes.

This is not evidence of changing the merchant population to improve results. It is a source-defined correction to keep persisted representations consistent. M1.10 then consumes these features to assess obligations, liquidity and capacity without regenerating the histories.

## Requirements interpretation

The following are editorial reading locators into the retained requirements/design/authority documents—not invented native requirement IDs. Native IDs and control lists remain in the linked originals.

**Design §3 · Time semantics.** Restrict every observation to the application as-of window and match scenario identities.

**Signoff · Annualized-sales identity.** Use the persisted rounded 90-day average and aligned availability.

**Design §§5–7 · Readiness and lineage.** Separate feature usability from creditworthiness and reconcile wide/long physical representations.

## What the retained validation establishes

Accepted v0.2R5 records 66/66 positives, 6/6 negatives and zero deterministic mismatches across 55,500 combined entities. The source reports 48,812 available and 5,188 unavailable long values; the wide states are 1,216 complete, 50 partial and 234 blocked. Availability counts are feature/snapshot populations, not final approvals.
