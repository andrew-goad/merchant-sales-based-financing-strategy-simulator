# M1.2 · Deterministic merchant population

**Can strategy comparisons hold the merchant universe constant?**

[Stage map](../../README.md#stage-map) · [Which source version?](../SOURCE_VERSIONS.md) · [Current use](../CURRENT_USE.md)

> **Source basis:** original `v0.2R2_ACCEPTED` in `Module_1/1.02_Deterministic_Merchant_Population`. This is the retained foundation/Module 1 basis; no Module 1 technical payload is changed by the R26 successor. This is a reading companion, not a new technical acceptance or execution guide.

A strategy comparison is difficult to interpret if the population changes at the same time. M1.2 creates one deterministic synthetic merchant universe that later requests, histories and scenarios reuse. Its job is identity and intrinsic/relationship context—not an underwriting verdict.

## Stable entities, different grains

The source creates 750 merchants, 1,347 owner/guarantor rows, 750 primary-industry assignments, five channel definitions, 750 processor accounts and 750 relationship snapshots. The **4,352 canonical entities** reconcile those distinct tables; they are not 4,352 customers.

Merchant keys follow `MSBF_POP_0001_M000001` through `MSBF_POP_0001_M000750`. Generation draws use population/merchant sequence, a deterministic seed version and stage-specific labels. Session-level `random()` is prohibited. G1 snapshots remain unchanged; code-owned supplementary assumptions have their own versioned specification and hash.

## Exact mixes without random population drift

The largest-remainder method multiplies each governed weight by 750, assigns the floor, then allocates residual rows by descending fractional remainder and category code. A deterministic ranking maps merchants into those exact quotas.

This has a useful interpretive consequence: an industry-share difference in a later output cannot be dismissed as a fresh random draw of the input population. Industry, geography, size, legal entity, relationship and channel mixes each have a retained target-versus-actual comparison.

## Coherent does not mean uniformly favorable

Business age and processor tenure are generated separately; processor tenure cannot precede formation. Ownership shares sum to 100%, with one to three owners. The synthetic owner score, derogatory flags, prior advances, repayment and relationship quality intentionally permit mixed signals.

The retained examples include strong-owner/adverse-relationship and weaker-owner/positive-relationship combinations. Those are synthetic design cases, not proof of empirical realism or calibrated borrower behavior. The stage must allow later evidence to complicate the initial profile rather than predetermine a favorable answer.

## What downstream receives

M1.3 receives stable merchant/relationship/channel context to generate requests. M1.4 receives operating attributes for daily histories. No application, POS, deposit, obligation, verification, feature, risk, expected-loss or contract rows are created here. In particular, guarantee availability on a synthetic owner record is not a completed offer guarantee or legal enforceability finding.

The source reconstructs expected canonical entities from frozen inputs and compares them with hashes recalculated from persisted table fields. Matching an aggregate row count alone is insufficient: every row and the population root must reconcile.

## Requirements interpretation

The following are editorial reading locators into the retained requirements/design/authority documents—not invented native requirement IDs. Native IDs and control lists remain in the linked originals.

**Design §§3–4 · Deterministic identity and governed mix.** Stable keys and largest-remainder quotas isolate population differences from later strategy changes.

**Design §§6–8 · Business, relationship and owners.** Enforce temporal coherence and ownership totals while retaining mixed signals.

**Design §9 · Canonical snapshot.** Compare the independently reconstructed persisted population, not only expected counts.

## What the retained validation establishes

Accepted v0.2R2 records 36/36 positive controls, 3/3 negative controls, 4,352 expected/actual entities and zero row mismatches. All 30 governed mix rows have zero deltas. The earlier canonical-formatting failure remains documented; acceptance applies to the corrected source/evidence, not the failed attempt. Upstream hashes and the empty downstream boundary were retained.
