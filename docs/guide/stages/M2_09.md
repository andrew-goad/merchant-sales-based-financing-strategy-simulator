## Certified state is a separate responsibility

M2.9 consumes the monitoring endpoint and servicing execution. It reconciles payment/event amounts, source exposure and resulting account state. M2.8 answers what execution was recorded; M2.9 answers whether those records reconcile to a defensible endpoint.

The current grain is the funded application/account under a scenario, with 130 records preserved. A review-held account remains an account with a balance and a documented state; it is not removed from the reconciliation population.

## Follow the balance bridge

The original design expresses the event relationship as scheduled amounts less returned amounts plus retry recovery, with sign and lineage conventions specified in the physical source. The net processing result is reconciled against source exposure; the post-servicing balance is not inferred from whether a status happens to contain the word “closed.”

The current retained bridge is:

```text
M2.5 monitoring-end exposure       $7,823.28
Less M2.8 processed servicing      $1,762.25
M2.10 certified ending exposure   $6,061.03
```

M2.9 supplies the account-state certification between execution and that portfolio result. These amounts describe the demonstrated scenario-path evidence, not a live receivables portfolio.

## Zero exceptions is not zero exposure

The accepted successor has zero unresolved payment/exposure reconciliation variances while retaining **$6,061.03** of certified ending exposure. That is possible because reconciliation asks whether records agree, not whether every account is paid off.

A horizon ending does not forgive the remaining receivable. Similarly, a held review route must retain the no-processing outcome and resulting balance rather than pretending a recommended action happened. These distinctions prevent a clean QA score from becoming an inaccurate commercial claim.

## Why M2.8's cents matter here

The preserved one-cent retry correction fixes the input arithmetic rather than adding tolerance to M2.9's exact-zero controls. Merchant 749's corrected execution total and retry now reconcile without concealing the original defect or changing unrelated balances.

The original v0.2R1 report contains a 59-path population and older residual amounts. The current R26 source carries 130 paths. The R17-to-R26 comparison has 22 shared M2.9 columns and no changed cells; this is the relevant downstream preservation statement, not a rerun of the old population.

## Consumer handoff

The stage produces reconciled account states, certification evidence and contract lineage for M2.10. The portfolio layer can then aggregate paid-off, active and review-held results using actual state semantics. It should not map a hard-coded dollar value to a status or estimate a certified balance from chart geometry.
