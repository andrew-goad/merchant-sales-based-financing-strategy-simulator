## A bounded comparison, not an autonomous strategy engine

M2.11 compares specified strategy profiles on a fixed, source-owned synthetic population. It preserves matched identities and reports the effects of permitted candidate selection and account-treatment rules. It does not prove production uplift, learn an optimal policy from real outcomes or deploy a selected strategy.

The original v0.2R13 SQL package is important lineage evidence. The current R26-integrated comparison uses the retained successor inputs and strategy-profile contract. Reading the original source under `src/current` does not make it the exact launcher for the later retained-final R26 method.

## Three populations, three denominators

| Evidence unit | Current population | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Candidate-profile evaluations | 6,519 × 8 = 52,152 | Each reportable structure assessed across eight profiles. |
| Application-profile simulations | 1,500 × 8 = 12,000 | Each application-scenario path retains a record per profile. |
| Account-profile simulations | 130 × 8 = 1,040 | The funded account-path population under each profile. |

The eight retained profiles are BASELINE_REPLAY, ACCESS_EXPANSION, PRICE_FOR_RISK, PAYMENT_BURDEN_RELIEF, LOSS_CONTAINMENT, PROFITABILITY_DISCIPLINE, EARLY_INTERVENTION and BALANCED_FRONTIER. They are source-configured alternatives, not eight macroeconomic scenarios. Baseline/Recession Energy remain a different axis.

## What a changed choice does—and does not—prove

The retained strategy analysis reports 249 selected-candidate changes and 247 amount changes. Its stated 65.5% candidate-selection rate belongs to the relevant candidate-bearing comparison population, not all 1,500 paths. A changed candidate can occur within an unchanged high-level decision class.

Indeed, the retained result shows **zero high-level decision migrations across the 1,500 application-scenario paths** for the reported comparison. Four of 1,040 account-profile simulations change treatment; exposure and payment-factor changes are not demonstrated in that account comparison. Terms/account-treatment sensitivity is therefore the accurate headline—not a demonstrated high-level decision challenger.

## The controls constrain the story

Candidate and source identities, policy profile, selection rules, scenario key, nonregression and output roots must remain comparable. The contract distinguishes source replay, weighted candidate selection and rule-based account treatment. A profile's economic preference does not grant permission to waive final authority or erase a hard boundary.

The accepted source supplies 24 portfolio-analysis rows across three reporting scopes and eight profiles. Counts of evaluation rows describe coverage; they are not thousands of independent governance controls or a commercial return estimate.
