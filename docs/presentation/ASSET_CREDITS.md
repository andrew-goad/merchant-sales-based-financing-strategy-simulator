# Presentation asset credits

[Presentation](README.md)

Narration and portfolio: **Andrew R. Goad**. The opening and closing cards use the established navy-and-teal presentation design and “No Cold Handoffs” branding. The [Human-in-the-Loop mark](assets/HITL_Mark.svg) was created for this presentation, with the person at the center of the loop.

The opening card adapts the author's [Human-in-the-Loop Analytical Leadership cover](https://github.com/andrew-goad/andrew-goad/blob/03cbea711f1b3d06ce1b77599286eb700dedb313/Human_in_the_Loop_Analytical_Leadership/Human_in_the_Loop_Analytical_Leadership.pdf): serif title, amber accents and a numbered right-hand column. Its three statements summarize the MCI demonstration: compare scenarios, explain the decision and follow the account.

Platform marks identify the tools or linked destinations. Their rights remain with their respective owners.
