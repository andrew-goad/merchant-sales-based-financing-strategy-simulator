# Project Roadmap

```text
G0 Physical Foundation
-> G1 Governed Run Control
-> M1.2-M1.14 Merchant, Operating, Risk & Economics Evidence
-> M1.15 Application Consumption Contract
```

## Next Authorized Stage - Module 2

Module 2 will consume the accepted `M1_G2_CONSUMPTION_BUNDLE v1` interface and add governed strategy and offer decisions:

- eligibility and fail-fast policy gates;
- candidate funding amount, remittance, duration, price, collateral, covenant, and protection structures;
- approval, counteroffer, manual review, decline, and alternative routing;
- transparent reason codes;
- acceptance elasticity and adverse-selection evidence;
- matched baseline/challenger strategy comparison;
- portfolio allocation and concentration controls;
- booked-contract lineage.

## Later Enterprise Horizons

### Module 3 - Portfolio & Relationship Management

Daily monitoring, dynamic lines, renewal, intervention, restructuring, loss mitigation, and relationship development.

### Module 4 - Enterprise Optimization & Learning

Portfolio optimization, channel-mix learning, stress propagation, capacity allocation, multi-generational strategy testing, and executive portfolio intelligence.

## Reporting Layer

Power BI is planned as a later validated executive and portfolio-intelligence layer. It is not represented as implemented in the Module 1 G2 v1.0.0 release.
